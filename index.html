ImgFlux Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective Date / 生效日期: 2026-11-25

English

  • No Data Collection: ImgFlux does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, telemetry, or advertising identifiers.
  • 100% Local Processing: All image conversion, compression, resizing, and EXIF metadata removal run strictly on your local computer. Your images are never uploaded to any server.
  • No Network Access: ImgFlux works completely offline and makes zero background network connections.
  • Local File Access: The app only accesses images and folders that you explicitly select or drop into the app, and saves converted results without overwriting original files. App preferences are stored locally in your Windows AppData folder.
